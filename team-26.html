Simple Reporting Guide

Goal: Get a basic Team 26 report in under 2 minutes
  1. Open your reporting tool.
  2. Choose a template: Team Summary or Sprint Summary (start simple).
  3. Filters: set Team = Team 26 and pick your Date range (e.g., Last 30 days).
  4. Columns: include Assignee, Status, Priority, Created, Resolved.
  5. Sort by Priority then Status.
  6. Click Run. If results look right, click Save as… and name it “T26 - Basics”.
Common errors (and quick fixes)
  • No results? Remove extra filters; confirm Team = Team 26 and date range covers the period.
  • Too many rows? Add a date range or filter Status to Open/In Progress/Done only.
  • Wrong columns? Edit columns and re-run; save again to keep your layout.
  • Need to share? Use Share and select your team or channel.
Starter report recipes
  • Work in progress (WIP): Team = Team 26, Status in (Open, In Progress), sort by Assignee.
  • Closed last week: Team = Team 26, Resolved in last 7 days, group by Assignee.
  • SLA health: Team = Team 26, Due date this week, show Priority and Owner.

FAQ

What should I choose when there are too many options?
Start with a template (Team or Sprint Summary), then only change Team, Date range, and Columns. That’s usually enough.
Can I get this as a CSV or PDF?
Yes—use Export in your reporting tool and pick CSV for spreadsheets or PDF for sharing.
